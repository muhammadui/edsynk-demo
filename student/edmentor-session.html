Refraction of Light

Physics · SS 1 · session 8 of 30

Practice · question 5 of 6

Nice work on the last one, Fatima. Now think about why light bends:

A ray travels from air into water at an angle. What happens to its speed and direction?

Water is denser than air, so light slows down — and slowing at an angle drags the ray towards the normal. That slowing is also why the straw in a glass looks bent.

Remember: denser medium → slower light → bends towards the normal.
