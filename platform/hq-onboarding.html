Onboarding playbook

Same 7 steps every school — done by us, not by them. That is the pitch.

1

Demo with their own artifacts

We mock THEIR report card live — school name, their grading, Arabic subjects if Islamiyyah.

2

Agreement + pilot terms

First term free for pilots. Price locked for 2 sessions. Export guarantee in writing.

3

Data intake

CSV, Excel, or photos of the register — we clean it. School never touches a spreadsheet.

4

School setup

Subdomain, grading scheme wizard, classes, subjects, arms, term dates.

5

Staff accounts

Bulk-create with access codes printed on cards for cheap-Android teachers.

6

Training, 90 minutes

Owner + exam officer + 2 teachers. Score entry on their own phones.

7

Dry-run publish, then live

One class, fake scores → publish → verify QR → withdraw. Then we go real.
