    Rules that override visibility for individual parents.

    Hide results when fees are owed

    When a parent has an outstanding balance, their child's result card is withheld in the portal and replaced with a notice to clear fees. The result itself is never deleted — it reappears the moment the balance is settled.

    Currently affects 32 parents with outstanding Term 3 balances.

    Require parent to verify phone before first login

    A one-time SMS code confirms the parent owns the registered number before any records are shown.
