FAQ

Questions schools ask us

Is my school's data safe?

Yes. Your data is encrypted, backed up daily, and never sold. Every result is cryptographically sealed and every change is audit-logged. We operate in line with Nigeria's NDPA 2023 — your school owns its data, full stop.

Can Islamic schools use Edsynk?

Absolutely — it's built for it. Islamiyya and hybrid schools get the Hijri calendar, a Saturday–Wednesday school week, Hifz and Tajweed tracking, and Arabic subjects rendered properly on the report card. No add-ons, included on every plan.

What about offline or poor network?

Edsynk is built for Nigerian networks. Teachers enter scores on their phones and the app saves through dropouts, syncing when signal returns. Result lookups and report cards work on any phone with a basic data connection.

Can we export our data?

Anytime. Export students, results and records to Excel or PDF whenever you want — even if you leave. Your data is yours; there is no lock-in.

How does billing work?

You pay per active student, once per term — or per session (the full year) at about 17% off. Your first term is a free pilot: we import your data and train your teachers before you pay anything. No per-school floor, no setup fee.
